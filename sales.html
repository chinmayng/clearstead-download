Privacy and local data storage

The finance tracker is a standalone HTML tool. Based on its current design, tracker entries are stored in the browser's local storage on the device where it is used; they are not automatically synced to a Clearstead account or another device. Clearing browser data or changing browsers may make those entries unavailable, so use the built-in export and backup options regularly.
